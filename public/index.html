What's new

v1.3.19 — 23 Nov 2026

  • The Locks tile padlock now animates: when the car unlocks, the shackle swings open; when it locks, it clicks shut.

v1.3.18 — 23 Nov 2026

  • Control icons are now the ones from your picture, extracted exactly — Flash lights is the headlight, Trunk is the car rear with the lid opening, Find car is the car signalling, and Windows is the car window. Locks and Charging kept their icons.

v1.3.17 — 23 Nov 2026

  • You can now set home yourself. Open any trip and tap "Set start as home" or "Set end as home" — that spot sticks as home (saved on the server) until you clear it in Settings → Home location. The auto-detected home no longer moves when the car sits somewhere else for days.

v1.3.16 — 23 Nov 2026

  • Fixed the trip history and home going wrong overnight. When the car is asleep, BYD reports a 0 km odometer and a (0, 0) GPS fix — and those were stored as real data, so home read [0, 0] (in the ocean) and phantom 23,728 km trips appeared. Those values are now ignored everywhere: new snapshots store them as missing, and the existing ones are filtered out when trips and home are worked out. Home is back to a real place.

v1.3.15 — 23 Nov 2026

  • When the car is charging, the hero card is now replaced by a charging scene: your side-view Dolphin photo, a big battery %, a pulsing glowing progress bar below the car, and the charger type — AC Charger, Fast Charger or Ultra Fast Charger, detected from the live charging power. The stats show Power (kW), Charged (kWh), Current (A) and Voltage (V). No cable, no charge box.

v1.3.14 — 22 Nov 2026

  • The Tyres top-view car is now your photo — the glass-roof top-down Dolphin, with its white background removed and the edges smoothed so the outline isn't jagged. The tyre pressures still point at the right wheels.
  • Home detection, corrected. The most-common-grid-cell approach still returned BSD live — your home longitude (107.15) sits exactly on a grid boundary, so GPS jitter split home across two cells and BSD won the count. Home is now the densest 1 km spot across snapshots (no grid, so no boundary to split on), which lands back in Cikarang.

v1.3.12 — 22 Nov 2026

  • Fixed the home detection that v1.3.11's "Home" label depends on. It took the median of all snapshots, which after your Cikarang → BSD drive landed in the middle of the route (~26 km from either end), so nothing was ever "Home". It now takes the densest 1 km spot across snapshots — the car is parked at home most of the time — so home is back in Cikarang. This also fixes trip start/end places showing "Home".

v1.3.11 — 22 Nov 2026

  • The location under the car name now updates. It was hardcoded to "Cikarang" — now it shows "Home" when the car is at home and the real area name (e.g. "BSD City") when it's away, from a reverse geocode of the car's GPS, cached so it doesn't re-fetch on every refresh.
  • The 6-digit BYD control PIN is now remembered on this device. Enter it once and it's auto-filled next time you unlock with the app key — no more re-entering it for every action. It's stored only in this browser, never sent to the server.

v1.3.10 — 22 Nov 2026

  • The scrollbar is hidden while scrolling — the page, the sheets and the trips table still scroll, the bar just never shows. Same pattern the quick-action pills already used.

v1.3.9 — 22 Nov 2026

  • Range is now calculated from the car's own measured efficiency (recent 50 km from its energy meter) instead of deriving efficiency from battery-% drops. The old way read 10.1 kWh/100km off a single short trip and gave 599 km full — the car's own meter says 14.1, which gives 429 km full, 403 km at 94%.

v1.3.8 — 22 Nov 2026

  • Regenerative braking no longer counts as charging. A snapshot only counts as charging when the charging gun is actually plugged in, and a charging session is dropped if the car drove during it. The live Charging tile uses the same rule, so it won't say "Charging" while you're braking.

v1.3.7 — 22 Nov 2026

  • Trips no longer split when the car sits still briefly mid-drive: segments separated by under 10 minutes (traffic light, quick stop) are now joined into one trip. A real stop — parked 10+ minutes — still counts as a separate trip.

v1.3.6 — 22 Nov 2026

  • Vehicle tab is now bento tiles: a wide Battery hero (big %, green bar, ≈ range), then Range, Range · your driving, Odometer, Efficiency · lifetime, Battery health · est., Capacity, and a wide Vehicle identity tile (Model, Nickname, VIN, Plate, Purchased). Your-driving range and battery health show "Collecting…" until there's enough history.

v1.3.5 — 22 Nov 2026

  • Controls tab: four new functions — Find car (horn + lights), Flash lights, Windows (tap to vent ~10% or close, shows Open/Closed) and Trunk (tap to open/close, shows Open/Closed). All need the 6-digit control PIN and ask first. The Climate tile is fixed too: the big temperature no longer clips at the tile edge, and the −/+ steppers are dark on white now.

v1.3.4 — 22 Nov 2026

  • The app can't be zoomed or copied now: pinch-zoom is locked, text can't be selected and the long-press copy menu is blocked. Input fields still accept typing and pasting.

v1.3.3 — 22 Nov 2026

  • Tariff and Battery kWh moved into Settings (••• → Settings → Energy & cost). Charging history is on the Energy page, and tapping any trip or charging card opens a detail sheet — Charge Detail has the charging curve chart, Trip details has the GPS route map.
  • Efficiency unit option in Settings: kWh/100km or km/kWh, everywhere at once (Energy, trips, Vehicle). Saved on this device.
  • Trips tab: Export CSV and Clear history moved into a ••• menu at the top right of the Trips card.

v1.3.2 — 22 Nov 2026

  • New app icon, from your photo: the orange-to-pink gradient square with the white star. It's now the login logo, the favicon, and the Add to Home Screen icon — replacing the green B.

v1.3.1 — 22 Nov 2026

  • Login screen, like HomeView: flat system background, round logo, "Sign in to BEYONDconnect", a 52px field with a dark border, and a black pill Sign in button (white in dark mode). No subtext.
  • New BEYONDconnect logo: a bold white B on a green gradient — the round version is the login logo, the rounded-square version is the favicon (and the Add to Home Screen icon).

v1.3.0 — 22 Nov 2026

  • A/C detail sheet: tap the Climate tile — seat ventilation for driver and passenger, big temperature slider, Fast cooling and Quick heating, circulation and fan speed.
  • Fixed the four screenshot issues: the Location map is a real map again, tyre pressures no longer hide behind the car, the hero car sits with breathing room, and the Climate −/+ buttons stay inside their tile.
  • Range is now estimated from your charging and trip history — on the Battery card, the Energy page and the Vehicle tab.
  • Trip history is detailed: each trip shows its route, duration, average speed, energy, efficiency, battery used and cost.
  • Wallpapers now show your actual photo (iOS was stretching them). More from HomeView: the date under the greeting, and quick-action pills (Lock, Climate, Charge).
  • Liquid Glass from HomeView: the dock, pills, sheets and buttons now have HomeView's top sheen, and the dock turns white in light mode with a quiet fill on the active tab.

v1.2.1 — 22 Nov 2026

  • Fix: the Vehicle tab's system-health section read fields BYD doesn't report, which broke the whole dashboard (black screen). The section is removed; the battery health estimate stays.

v1.2.0 — 22 Nov 2026

  • Home screen redesigned: greeting, dark hero card with your Atlantis Grey Dolphin photo, Battery + Last trip cards, full-bleed location map.
  • New Vehicle tab: VIN, plate, purchase date, odometer, battery health estimate.
  • Backgrounds: your five wallpapers (Ember, Dune, Nebula, Silk, Beams) with frosted glass cards; Control PIN moved into Settings.
  • Battery corrected to 60.48 kWh (Extended Range).
  • Top bar removed; the ••• menu now sits next to the greeting.

v1.1.0 — 22 Nov 2026

  • Remote controls: lock / unlock, climate start/stop (temp + duration), start charging. 6-digit control PIN, once per tab, never stored.
  • Background trip logging: the car is polled every 5 min; Trips shows automatic trips with distance, energy and cost.
  • Page-based navigation with a floating glass bottom dock.
  • Tyre pressure now shows your own top-down car photo.
  • Liquid Glass restyle: frosted unlock card, glass pill buttons, capsule on the active tab.
  • Charging can be started but not stopped from the app — stop with the key fob / door-handle double-unlock.

v1.0.0 — 22 Nov 2026

  • First release: live battery, range, odometer, charging, location, tyres, doors, climate, energy and cost from BYD's Indonesia server.
  • No Google sign-in, no subscription, no account system. App-key protected.
  • Liquid Glass design, OLED dark mode, charging scene with live power and ETA.
