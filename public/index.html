What's new

v1.2.1 — 22 Nov 2026

  • Fix: the Vehicle tab's system-health section read fields BYD doesn't report, which broke the whole dashboard (black screen). The section is removed; the battery health estimate stays.

v1.2.0 — 22 Nov 2026

  • Home screen redesigned: greeting, dark hero card with your Atlantis Grey Dolphin photo, Battery + Last trip cards, full-bleed location map.
  • New Vehicle tab: VIN, plate, purchase date, odometer, battery health estimate.
  • Backgrounds: your five wallpapers (Ember, Dune, Nebula, Silk, Beams) with frosted glass cards; Control PIN moved into Settings.
  • Battery corrected to 60.48 kWh (Extended Range).
  • Top bar removed; the ••• menu now sits next to the greeting.

v1.1.0 — 22 Nov 2026

  • Remote controls: lock / unlock, climate start/stop (temp + duration), start charging. 6-digit control PIN, once per tab, never stored.
  • Background trip logging: the car is polled every 5 min; Trips shows automatic trips with distance, energy and cost.
  • Page-based navigation with a floating glass bottom dock.
  • Tyre pressure now shows your own top-down car photo.
  • Liquid Glass restyle: frosted unlock card, glass pill buttons, capsule on the active tab.
  • Charging can be started but not stopped from the app — stop with the key fob / door-handle double-unlock.

v1.0.0 — 22 Nov 2026

  • First release: live battery, range, odometer, charging, location, tyres, doors, climate, energy and cost from BYD's Indonesia server.
  • No Google sign-in, no subscription, no account system. App-key protected.
  • Liquid Glass design, OLED dark mode, charging scene with live power and ETA.
