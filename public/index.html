What this is

A free, single-user clone of BYD Connect's live dashboard — no Google sign-in, no subscription, no account system. It talks straight to BYD's Indonesia server for your Dolphin. Read-only in v1: remote lock / climate and scheduled charging need your BYD control PIN, and background trip logging while the page is closed needs a server that polls — both are next, not in this build.
